How this works
  1. Snap a cover — photograph the front sleeve and save. You don't have to type anything; the record lands here marked Needs details.
  2. Claude fills the pending ones in overnight — artist, title, label, catalogue number, year, genres and tracklist — from the photo.
  3. Or tap + Add record and type it in yourself. Add a row per mix so each one carries its own BPM.

The BPM filter matches any mix on a record, so 126–130 will surface a record whose B-side sits in that range.

Adding and editing needs the key set once per phone, under the gear. Without it the crate is read-only.
